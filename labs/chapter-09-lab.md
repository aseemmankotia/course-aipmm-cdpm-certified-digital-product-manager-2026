# Chapter 9 Lab: Growth Experimentation, Pricing, and Product-Led Growth

**Estimated time:** 18 minutes

## Exercise

Given funnel data showing signup, activation, and paid-conversion rates across three stages, identify the largest drop-off stage, propose one growth experiment targeting that stage, and define its success metric

## Key takeaway

Diagnose the specific funnel stage with the largest drop-off before designing a growth experiment, and match the growth motion and pricing model to the product's price point and buying behavior.
