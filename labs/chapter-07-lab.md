# Chapter 7 Lab: Prioritization Frameworks and Outcome-Based Roadmapping

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Given a list of eight backlog candidates with reach/impact/effort estimates, calculate RICE scores, then re-rank the same list using WSJF logic and explain where the two rankings diverge and why

## Key takeaway

Match the prioritization framework to the constraint described — urgency favors WSJF, feature-type expectation favors Kano, and outcome-based roadmaps avoid fixed dates.
