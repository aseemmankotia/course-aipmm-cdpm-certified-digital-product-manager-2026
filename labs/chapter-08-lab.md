# Chapter 8 Lab: Product Analytics Foundations and the North Star Metric

**Estimated time:** 18 minutes

## Exercise

Given a sample analytics dashboard with total signups, day-7 retention, and weekly active users, identify which metric is vanity, which is actionable, propose a North Star metric, and define one guardrail metric

## Key takeaway

A North Star metric and its guardrails must reflect ongoing value delivery, not vanity counts of acquisition activity.
