# Chapter 2 Lab: Customer Discovery, JTBD, and Design Thinking — Understanding the User

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Conduct a mock JTBD interview using the provided script, extract functional/emotional/social jobs from the transcript, and rewrite three user stories as job stories

## Key takeaway

JTBD and Design Thinking exist to validate the right problem before any solution is designed, tested, or built.
