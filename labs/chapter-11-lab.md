# Chapter 11 Lab: Responsible AI, Platform Thinking, and Security for Digital Products

**Estimated time:** 18 minutes

## Exercise

Given a proposed AI feature brief (auto-approving loan applications using a model), complete a responsible-AI risk checklist covering data provenance, bias risk, human-in-the-loop requirement, and disclosure to end users

## Key takeaway

Choose ML only when data and stakes justify it, always evaluate non-deterministic output risk against the decision's impact, and apply human-in-the-loop and platform integration safeguards for high-risk digital features.
